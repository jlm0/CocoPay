🚪 Token Holder Behavior

What do token holders do with their tokens each month? These must sum to 100%.

Burn tokens → get USDC. Highest fee path (~14% at 10% tax).

Borrow USDC against tokens. ~6% fee (2.5% JB + 2.5% source + 1% REV).

Keep tokens in wallet. No fees, but no liquidity.

🔄 Community Commerce

The key to viability: tokens spent at OTHER stores in the network instead of exiting.

% of potential exits redirected to purchases at other Cocopay stores. 0% fee (just token transfer).

Why this matters: High community commerce = lower effective fees. At 50%+ network spend, Cocopay can compete with Stripe's 3.2%.
